## What Is This Repository?

This is your personal, semester-long **Investigative Workbench**. Think of this repository as your digital reporter's notebook, laboratory bench, and evidentiary safe.

Here, you will:
1. **Maintain your chain of custody:** Log digital evidence, timestamps, archive URLs, and cryptographic hashes in [`investigation-log.md`](investigation-log.md).
2. **Audit AI interactions:** Track every generative AI prompt, output, hallucination check, and critique in [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md).
3. **Store computational code:** Save your Python web scrapers, data cleaning scripts, and Jupyter notebooks.
4. **Draft course performance tasks:** Build your four core semester investigations:
   - Task 1: Job Posting Vetting
   - Task 2: Health Misinformation Debunk
   - Task 3: Video & Geolocation Forensic Analysis
   - Task 4: Summative Capstone Investigation

## Verification & Archival Rules

- **Never rely on a single source:** Triangulate every factual assertion across at least 2 independent vectors.
- **Preserve transient links immediately:** Before analyzing a social media post or forum thread, save a permanent snapshot to [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/save) or [Archive.today](https://archive.today).
- **Audit every AI output:** Treat LLM outputs as unverified leads from an anonymous source. Verify all names, dates, and claims.
- **Protect privacy:** Do not commit non-public personally identifiable information (PII) or unredacted sensitive documents.
